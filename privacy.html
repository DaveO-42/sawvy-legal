2. GRUNDSATZ: LOKALE DATENSPEICHERUNG

Sawvy speichert alle von dir eingegebenen Projektdaten – Projekte, Bretter, Teile und Einstellungen – ausschließlich lokal auf deinem Gerät. Diese Inhalte werden von Sawvy nicht an eigene oder fremde Server übertragen.

Zusätzlich kann Android die App-Daten – abhängig von deinen Systemeinstellungen – über die Funktion „Automatische Sicherung" in dein persönliches Google Drive sichern. Diese Sicherung erfolgt durch das Betriebssystem, nicht durch Sawvy: sie liegt in deinem eigenen Google-Konto, zählt nicht auf dein Drive-Kontingent und ist bei eingerichteter Bildschirmsperre Ende-zu-Ende-verschlüsselt, sodass auch Google die Inhalte nicht lesen kann. Du kannst die Funktion jederzeit systemweit unter Einstellungen →Google → Sicherung deaktivieren.

Mit deiner Einwilligung nutzen wir Firebase Crashlytics zur Absturzerkennung – Details, welche Daten dabei verarbeitet werden und wie du widersprechen kannst, findest du in Abschnitt 4.

Du kannst deine Entscheidung jederzeit in den App-Einstellungen unter „Absturzberichte teilen" ändern.

2. PRINCIPLE: LOCAL DATA STORAGE

Sawvy stores all project data you enter – projects, boards, parts, and settings – exclusively on your device. Sawvy does not transmit this content to its own or any third-party servers.

In addition, depending on your system settings, Android may back up the app's data to your personal Google Drive via the "Automatic Backup" feature. This backup is performed by the operating system, not by Sawvy: it resides in your own Google account, does not count against your Drive quota, and is end-to-end encrypted when a screen lock is set, so not even Google can read its contents. You can disable the feature system-wide at any time under Settings → Google → Backup.

With your consent, we use Firebase Crashlytics for crash detection – see Section 4 for details on what data is processed and how to opt out.

3. IN-APP PURCHASES (REVENUECAT)

For Sawvy Pro purchases, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., 633 Tasman St, San Leandro, CA 94577, USA). RevenueCat processes purchase data solely to validate and manage in-app purchases via the Google Play Store.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Purchases are processed through the Google Play Store: policies.google.com/privacy

4. CRASH REPORTING (FIREBASE CRASHLYTICS)

Sawvy uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to detect app crashes and improve stability.

Crashlytics is disabled by default and only becomes active once you explicitly consent on first app launch. If you consent, the following technical data is sent to Google servers: device model, OS version, app version, and error logs (stack traces). Your project content (measurements, labels, boards, parts) is not collected.

You can change your decision at any time in the app settings under "Share crash reports".

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. DATA OVERVIEW

  • Via RevenueCat: Purchase status for Sawvy Pro (no personal content)
  • With consent via Firebase Crashlytics: Device model, OS version, app version, error logs
  • Via Android Auto Backup (system-level, can be disabled): project data and settings in the user's own Google Drive
  • Not collected: Name, email, location, contacts

If Android's automatic backup is active, a copy remains in your Google Drive and will be restored on reinstallation; you can delete that backup under Settings → Google → Backup. You can withdraw your Firebase Crashlytics consent at any time in the app settings.

For data stored by RevenueCat, contact gdapps@proton.me or RevenueCat directly.
