4. ABSTURZBERICHTE (FIREBASE CRASHLYTICS)

Sawvy nutzt Firebase Crashlytics (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Irland), um Programmabstürze zu erkennen und die App-Stabilität zu verbessern.

Crashlytics ist standardmäßig deaktiviert und wird erst aktiv, wenn du beim ersten Start der App ausdrücklich zustimmst. Bei Zustimmung werden folgende technische Daten an Google-Server übertragen: Gerätemodell, Betriebssystemversion, App-Version und Fehlerprotokolle (Stack-Traces). Deine Projektinhalte (Maße, Bezeichnungen, Bretter, Teile) werden dabei nicht erfasst.

Du kannst deine Entscheidung jederzeit in den App-Einstellungen unter „Absturzberichte teilen" ändern.

Datenschutzerklärung von Google: policies.google.com/privacy

2. PRINCIPLE: LOCAL DATA STORAGE

Sawvy stores all project data you enter – projects, boards, parts, and settings – exclusively on your device. This content is never transmitted to external servers.

With your consent, we use Firebase Crashlytics for crash detection – see Section 4 for details on what data is processed and how to opt out.

3. IN-APP PURCHASES (REVENUECAT)

For Sawvy Pro purchases, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., 633 Tasman St, San Leandro, CA 94577, USA). RevenueCat processes purchase data solely to validate and manage in-app purchases via the Google Play Store.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Purchases are processed through the Google Play Store: policies.google.com/privacy

4. CRASH REPORTING (FIREBASE CRASHLYTICS)

Sawvy uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to detect app crashes and improve stability.

Crashlytics is disabled by default and only becomes active once you explicitly consent on first app launch. If you consent, the following technical data is sent to Google servers: device model, OS version, app version, and error logs (stack traces). Your project content (measurements, labels, boards, parts) is not collected.

You can change your decision at any time in the app settings under "Share crash reports".

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. DATA OVERVIEW

  • Stored locally: Project data, boards, parts, settings (unit, blade width)
  • Via RevenueCat: Purchase status for Sawvy Pro (no personal content)
  • With consent via Firebase Crashlytics: Device model, OS version, app version, error logs
  • Not collected: Name, email, location, contacts

6. APP PERMISSIONS

  • INTERNET: Used to validate in-app purchases via RevenueCat and Google Play, and – if you consent – to transmit crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics.

No other permissions (camera, location, contacts, etc.) are requested.

7. DATA DELETION

All locally stored data is deleted when you uninstall the app. Individual projects can be deleted at any time within the app.

You can withdraw your Firebase Crashlytics consent at any time in the app settings.

For data stored by RevenueCat, contact [email-address] or RevenueCat directly.
